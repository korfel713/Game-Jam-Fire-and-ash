# Fire and Ash

Fire and Ash is a 3D game made in Godot for the Respawn Game Jam Spring 2026 at Florida Polytechnic University.

You play as a living fire trying to burn through a house. Spreading to flammable objects keeps the fire alive, while explosive objects can create larger chain reactions and fire extinguishers can stop the spread.

The game was built over a game-jam weekend by Matthew Rigg, Megan Ruiz, David Thompson, and Sean Thompson.

## Gameplay

The player controls the fire directly and moves through the environment setting nearby flammable objects alight. Burning objects provide fuel that helps keep the fire alive. Some objects can trigger explosions and spread the fire farther, while fire extinguishers work against the player by suppressing nearby flames.

The project includes multiple levels, menus, audio controls, a pause/end flow, and a credits sequence.
